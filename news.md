This page records verifiable developments in the Navi Musaget literary and research archive. It is an archival chronology, not a release catalogue. Dates are stated as precisely as the available records support.

## 2026

### November 2026 — Completed manuscript

**WHAT IF I'M A GOAT?** — a completed English-language collection of four works of speculative fiction: *BASIC PACKAGE*, *THE GOAT, NAVIN, AND AI*, *HOW I MEASURED MEANING IN IPS*, and *PROBLEM 51*. Publication of the collection is under consideration.

[Collection record](what-if-im-a-goat.html)

### 2026 — External publication

**PROBLEM 51** by Navi Musaget appeared as the feature story in Issue One of *From Mind and Machine*, an independent magazine publishing creative work at the boundary of human authorship and artificial intelligence.

[Publication record](problem-51-from-mind-and-machine.html) · [Magazine issue](https://ashadowfromthetomb.com/from-mind-and-machine/issue-1/magazine/)

### 2026 — Literary award

**DENIED ACCESS** by Navi Musaget was named **Winner, Science Fiction (On Earth)** in the 2026 Next Generation Indie Book Awards.

[Official winners list](https://indiebookawards.com/winners.php) · [Works archive](works.html)
