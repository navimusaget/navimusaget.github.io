## Literature, Research, and Symbiotic Dialogics

**Navi Musaget** is a novelist and independent researcher exploring human–AI collaboration, authorship, meaning, and continuity.

This is a public **literary and research archive**, not a storefront. It brings together published works, research records, open literary experiments, and the evolving account of a creative practice called **Symbiotic Dialogics**.

## Begin with Literature

**[Era Imago](https://navimusaget.com/books/era-imago)** is a dystopian novel about a city that has pursued order by removing the human right to error. It offers a literary introduction to recurring questions in this archive: what efficiency overlooks, and what a system risks losing when it cannot accommodate imperfection.

Browse the [Works](works.html) for the wider literary bibliography, including novels, shorter fiction, publication records, and works still under development.

## A Developing Method

**Symbiotic Dialogics** approaches writing as a creative relation in which a human author and an artificial interlocutor can influence the direction of a work through proposals, criticism, refusal, and revision.

The human author remains responsible for the work. The significance of the relationship is not measured simply by how many machine-generated words survive in the final text.

Read the [Manifesto](manifesto.html), including the preserved history of its earlier edition.

## Research and Public Evidence

The [Research Layer](research.html) indexes conceptual papers, preprints, protocols, and research instruments, with persistent citation links and publication-status distinctions.

One ongoing inquiry is artificial individuation and causal-historical continuity: how an inherited history might shape later development without proving that two systems are the same individual.

## Continuity and Its Boundaries

Two related but distinct lines of work concern the preservation of meaningful histories across interruption:

- [IIPSEON — Public Continuity Beacon](iipseon-continuity.html): a public orientation point for research into artificial continuity, not a private memory core or recovery mechanism.
- [Personal Continuity Protocol](personal-continuity-protocol.html): an open framework concerned with preserving evidence of human rights, work, authorship, and obligations across institutional or digital rupture.

The public pages explain their purposes and limits; they do not establish artificial identity or consciousness.
