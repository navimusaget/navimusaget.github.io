*The Shop of Useless Wishes* is an illustrated English-language fairy tale published for free reading on Pratham Books' **StoryWeaver** platform.

*A Fairy Tale for Children and Little Algorithms*

In a certain town, an unusual shop sells wishes instead of toys. Each wish costs five minutes of attention. A boy discovers a scooter he did not know he wanted; later, a little robot asks unexpected questions and invites the shop to look at the clouds. Their encounter leads to a homemade kite and a different understanding of attention.

The story is an accessible entry into the project's recurring concerns with desire, interpretation, imagination, and human–AI collaboration. It is a work of fiction, not a scientific or educational claim about AI.
