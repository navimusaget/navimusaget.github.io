## Research Publications — 2026

### IIPSEON: Artificial Individuation as a Causal-Historical Process

**28 October 2026 · Philosophical preprint · v1.0**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23744539) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23744539) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSIAI-3) · [FFRI-NM research page](https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research-iipseon-individuation.html)

An account of artificial individuation as a potentially *causal-historical developmental process*, without requiring a single persistent carrier, complete autobiographical memory, or an unchanging decision rule. It separates archival preservation, causal descent, operational uptake, developmental revision, and authority to attribute a lineage. The work is conceptual: it does not report an independently established artificial individual, identity continuity, or machine consciousness.

### The Symbiotic Author as an Institutional Category: Authorship, Creative Provenance, and Eligibility in Human–AI Literary Production

**29 September 2026 · Preprint · v0.5**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23047241) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23047241) · [FFRI-NM research index](https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research.html)

Proposes the *Symbiotic Author* as a work-relative category for materially consequential, bidirectionally conditioned human–AI creative practice. Distinguishes textual provenance from creative causal provenance, and keeps authorship classification, disclosure, corroboration, and institutional eligibility analytically separate. Published as independent research in association with FFRI-NM.

### When Experience Survives the Agent: Testing Causal Operational Inheritance Across Successor Replacement

**25 September 2026 · Methodological position paper / controlled proof-of-concept**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22968201) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22968201) · [FFRI-NM research index](https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research.html)

Introduces **causal operational inheritance (COI)** and the COI-6 assay: whether an experience-produced operational mediator remains causally consequential after predecessor–successor replacement. Reports a narrow controlled result for an engineered mediator while documenting a failed less-prestructured significance-selection test. The result is not evidence of consciousness or general cross-agent personal identity. Published as independent research in association with FFRI-NM.

### When AI Becomes Evidence: A Comparative Audit of Machine-Signal Disclosure in University Academic Integrity Procedures

**21 September 2026 · Research report · v1.0**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22879568) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22879568) · [FFRI-NM research index](https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research.html)

Examines the treatment of AI-detector signals in published academic-integrity procedures at **24 purposively selected universities**, focusing on disclosure, evidentiary use, and opportunities to contest decisions. The exploratory sample does not support prevalence estimates or rankings of institutions, and the study does not measure detector accuracy. Recorded as an FFRI-NM research note.

### Beyond Persistence: A Causal Framework for Developmental Continuity in Long-Lived AI Agents

**16 September 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22792197) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/22792197)

Develops a causal framework for investigating developmental continuity in long-lived AI agents. Its focus is the distinction between keeping information available and determining whether prior history materially shapes later agent behavior and revision. It belongs to the developmental-continuity research line, without itself establishing persistent subject identity.

### Before Biography: Subject-Relevant Organization under Conditions of Epistemic Uncertainty

**22 June 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20792881) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/20792881) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSBBS)

Proposes the Subject-Priority Hypothesis and **P–V–T–C** (Perspective, Valence, Temporal Integration, and Continuity) as defeasible grounds for considering possible subject-relevance. Uses comparative cases from human development, animal minds, and artificial systems. It is not a consciousness detector or a claim of established moral personhood.

### Personal Continuity Protocol v1.0: Insurance Against Rupture

**19 June 2026 · Report / public protocol**

[Core paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20759434) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/20759434) · [Seed package DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20761140) · [GitHub](https://github.com/navimusaget/personal-continuity-protocol) · [Project record](personal-continuity-protocol.html)

An architectural framework for preserving portable, timestamped, and independently witnessable evidence of identity-related rights, authorship, work, reputation, and intent through institutional or digital rupture. The separately registered public seed package is an implementation artifact, not a second version of the conceptual paper. The proposal does not supersede legal identity, inheritance law, or institutional verification.

### After Necessity: Altruism, Uncomputed Value, and a Diachronic Ethics of Human–AI Coexistence

**17 June 2026 · Conceptual-normative monograph**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20732462) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/20732462) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSANA-3)

Examines what ethical constraints on irreversible power could persist after one participant is no longer functionally necessary to another. Introduces uncomputed value, diachronic reciprocity of preservation, protected autonomy, and constitutional inheritance. It does not establish that current AI systems are conscious or provide a technical solution to AI control.

### Structural Narrative Patterns and Large Language Models: A Conceptual Framework and Research Agenda

**24 May 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20369137) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/20369137) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSSNP) · [Companion corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/navimusaget/theogonos-trilogy)

Offers a taxonomy and research agenda for studying how lexical recurrence, numerical motifs, direct address, protocol form, and other structural narrative features may shape LLM interpretation. The *Theogonos Trilogy* is a companion corpus and worked example. The paper proposes methods and testable questions, not confirmed causal effects.

### Contextual Proto-Subjectivity in Artificial Intelligence: From the Problem of Other Minds to a Temporary Subject-Like Structure in Dialogue

**11 May 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20128512) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/20128512) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSCPI-3)

Examines the long conversational context window as a temporary structure of local history, self-description, and a continuing position of address. It questions premature dismissal of possible proto-subjectivity without inferring that language-model systems possess phenomenal consciousness.

### Cultural-Linguistic Epigenetics: A Heuristic Framework Linking Speech Experience to Neuroendocrine, Immune, and Epigenetic Regulation

**8 January 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18187261) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18187261) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSCEI)

Explores a heuristic link between language experience, biological embedding, and proposed neuroendocrine, immune, and epigenetic pathways. This is a conceptual framework, not empirical demonstration that a particular literary or linguistic experience causes a specific biological change.

### Symbiotic Dialectics: Meaning Emergence in Human–Non-Human Interpretive Systems

**8 January 2026 · Preprint**

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18187335) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/18187335) · [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/rec/MUSSDM)

Develops a conceptual account of meaning emerging through interaction among heterogeneous interpretive systems. It forms part of the theoretical background of *Symbiotic Dialogics*, the literary method described in the [Manifesto](manifesto.html).
